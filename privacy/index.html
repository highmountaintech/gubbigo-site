1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how the GubbiGo Indian language-learning app (“we”, “us”) handles information.

The app works offline. On each install it creates a Firebase account id so progress can be saved. You do not have to sign in. If you choose Save progress with Google, Google sends that account’s name and email to Firebase Authentication. The app shows the email. The name and email are not copied into the lesson-progress document. GubbiGo has no payments: there is nothing to buy in the app, and we do not collect payment details.

2. Summary

Lesson progress stays on your device and, when you are online, syncs to your Firebase account. We do not collect a payment card or UPI ID, because there is nothing to buy. If you save progress with Google, Firebase Authentication stores that Google account’s name and email. Lessons use seeds, which refill over time. When you are out of seeds you can watch an optional rewarded ad for one more. The Home screen also shows a banner ad. Ads come from Google AdMob and are requested as non-personalised and child-directed. The app downloads language packs when you need them and may check Expo for an app update.

This policy covers:

  • progress stored on the device;
  • the Firebase account and the progress saved for it;
  • technical data processed by Google Play, Firebase (including App Check), Google Sign-In, Google AdMob, Expo, our email provider, and our content host.

3. Information stored on your device

GubbiGo keeps a local database on your phone or tablet so you can learn offline. This typically includes:

  • lesson and exercise progress, scores, and completion status;
  • the language you speak and the language you are learning;
  • streaks, seed balance, and collected characters;
  • downloaded lesson content and audio stored for playback;
  • how many optional rewarded ads were watched today. This count stays on the device. It is not part of the saved progress document.

We do not send lesson progress or your email to build an advertising profile.

4. Account and saved progress

Every install receives a Firebase account id. Until you choose Google, that account is anonymous: it has no name and no email. When the device is online, the app saves a progress document for that id. The document can include:

  • the language you speak and the language you are learning;
  • streaks, the last active day, and the daily goal;
  • the seed balance and when seeds last refilled;
  • lesson and exercise results;
  • collected characters.

The progress document does not include your name, email, or how many ads you watched.

Save progress with Google is optional. If you use it, Google Sign-In gives Firebase the name and email of that Google account. The app shows the email on the Profile screen (Home menu → Profile). The name and email stay on the Firebase account. They are not copied into the progress document. You do not type a password into GubbiGo. If that Google account already has progress, the app asks before merging this device’s lessons into it. After a reinstall, signing in with the same Google account restores that progress.

Signing out first saves your latest progress to your Google account when it can, then clears lesson progress on this device and leaves the saved progress in Firebase. If it cannot save (for example you are offline), the app warns you before you choose to sign out anyway. Delete account on the Profile screen is available after you have saved progress with Google. It asks you to confirm with Google, then deletes the progress document, deletes the Firebase account, and clears lesson progress on this device. A new empty anonymous account then starts on the device.

Anonymous accounts. If you never save progress with Google, your account stays anonymous. We cannot link it to a person, so it cannot be restored after you uninstall, and we cannot look it up to delete it on request. We are introducing automatic deletion of anonymous accounts that have not been used for a long time (we plan 90 days without opening the app), together with their saved progress. Until that is switched on, an unused anonymous account and its saved progress remain stored. We will update this page when it is live.

6. Seeds and payments

GubbiGo has no payments. There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases, or paid unlocks, and the app has no way to pay. We do not collect, store, or see a payment card number, UPI ID, or store-account password, and no payment provider receives your information from the app.

Lessons use seeds. You hold up to five. Starting a lesson uses one, and one comes back every 40 minutes. When you have none, you can wait for a seed to refill or watch one optional rewarded ad for one seed, up to three ads a day. The seed balance is saved with your progress. The number of ads you watched today stays on the device.

If we ever add payments, we will update this policy and the Terms of Use before they are offered.

7. Advertising

GubbiGo shows ads from Google AdMob in two places: a banner on the Home screen, and an optional rewarded ad offered when you are out of seeds. No advertisement appears during a lesson or between exercises. A rewarded ad plays only if you choose to watch it. The reward is one seed, up to three a day, and skipping it leaves the lesson and the seed balance unchanged.

Ad requests are marked as child-directed, ask for non-personalised ads only, and are limited to content rated G. Where required, Google’s consent message is shown before any ad is requested. If you do not give consent where it is required, no ads are requested and the app works without them.

We do not sell your information. We do not give AdMob your name, email, lesson scores, or saved progress.

8. Children

GubbiGo is made for children learning with a parent or guardian nearby, and for adults starting a new language. The app does not include chat, social features, or public profiles, and it does not ask anyone to type a name.

An install still creates a Firebase account id, and lesson progress can sync to it. The progress document does not include a name or email. If someone chooses Save progress with Google, that Google account’s name and email are stored on the Firebase account, so a parent or guardian should be the one to sign in with Google. Ads are requested as child-directed, non-personalised, and G-rated, and we send AdMob no name, email, or lesson progress. A parent can decide whether a child watches an optional rewarded ad.

If you believe we have received personal information from a child that we should not have, email developer@xlang.in and we will delete it if it is in our control.

9. App permissions

GubbiGo is built to use:

  • Internet — to download language packs, save and restore progress, sign in with Google, check for app updates, and load ads;
  • Audio playback — to play lesson and word audio. The app does not record audio;
  • Advertising — the ad SDK may read device information needed to deliver and measure ads, which are requested as non-personalised;
  • Local storage — to keep progress, downloaded lessons and audio, and the day’s rewarded-ad count.

GubbiGo does not request microphone, camera, contacts, or precise location permission for these features.

10. Retention and deletion

  • On-device lessons: remain until you sign out, delete the account, clear app storage, or uninstall. Signing out or deleting a Google-linked account clears lesson progress on this device.
  • Cloud progress, Google account: stays in Firebase until you delete the account in the app, or we delete it after a Delete my data request we can match. Signing out leaves the Google account’s saved progress in place.
  • Cloud progress, anonymous account: kept until it is deleted. We are introducing automatic deletion, together with the account, after a period without opening the app (planned: 90 days) and will update this page when it is live. Reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone may start a new anonymous account; the old one remains until it is deleted.
  • Google name and email: stay on the Firebase account until that account is deleted. They are not stored in the progress document.
  • Delete-my-data requests: the email and optional note you submit on the delete-data page are kept only long enough to process the request, and are automatically purged no later than 60 days after submission whether or not the request was confirmed.
  • Rewarded-ad count: stays on the device and resets when lesson progress on the device is cleared. It is not saved in Firebase. Google may keep its own ad measurement records under its policy.
  • Download and update logs: Expo or Cloudflare may retain ordinary request logs under their policies.

11. Your choices and rights

You can:

  • learn using the seeds on the device, and skip the optional ad;
  • skip Google sign-in and keep the anonymous account;
  • turn off network access; lessons already on the device still work offline;
  • clear app data or uninstall to remove local progress;
  • delete a Google-linked account from the Profile screen, which removes that account and its saved progress;
  • use the Delete my data form (or email developer@xlang.in with the subject “Delete my data”) to ask what information we hold or to request deletion of an account we can match. Steps are on Support.

Depending on where you live (including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act and, where applicable, GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, or to withdraw consent. We will help where we can.

We will update this page if we add other ad placements, payments, analytics, or a new category of data. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Material changes will also be reflected in the store listing where required.

13. Contact

Please do not send passwords to this address.
