1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how the GubbiGo Indian language-learning app (“we”, “us”) handles information.

The app works offline. On each install it creates a Firebase account id so progress can be saved. You do not have to sign in. If you choose Save progress with Google, Google sends that account’s email to Firebase Authentication. We do not operate our own payment servers. Google Play bills Premium and seed packs. On an App Store build, Apple bills those purchases. RevenueCat tells the app whether Premium is active.

2. Summary

Lesson progress stays on your device and, when you are online, syncs to your Firebase account. We do not collect your name, payment card, or UPI ID. We collect a Google email only if you save progress with Google. Lessons use seeds. Seeds refill over time, seed packs add more, and Premium is an optional subscription for unlimited seeds and no ads. Without Premium, the app can offer an optional rewarded ad in exchange for one seed. The app downloads language packs when you need them and may check Expo for an app update.

This policy covers:

  • progress stored on the device, including a record of seed-pack orders;
  • the Firebase account and the progress saved for it;
  • purchase and technical data processed by Google Play, Apple (on an App Store build), RevenueCat, Firebase, Google Sign-In, Google AdMob, Expo, and our content host.

3. Information stored on your device

GubbiGo keeps a local database on your phone or tablet so you can learn offline. This typically includes:

  • lesson and exercise progress, scores, and completion status;
  • the language you speak and the language you are learning;
  • streaks, seed balance, and collected characters;
  • downloaded lesson content and audio stored for playback;
  • whether Premium is active, cached after a successful check so unlimited seeds still work offline;
  • the Google Play order id for a seed pack, kept so that purchase is not granted again. This record stays on the device after you sign out or delete the account. Uninstalling the app removes it.
  • how many optional rewarded ads were watched today. This count stays on the device. It is not part of the saved progress document.

We do not send lesson progress or your email to build an advertising profile. Premium status is not written into the saved progress document.

4. Account and saved progress

Every install receives a Firebase account id. Until you choose Google, that account is anonymous: it has no name and no email. When the device is online, the app saves a progress document for that id. The document can include:

  • the language you speak and the language you are learning;
  • streaks, the last active day, and the daily goal;
  • the seed balance and when seeds last refilled;
  • lesson and exercise results;
  • collected characters.

The progress document does not include your name, email, payment details, Premium status, Play order ids, or how many ads you watched.

Save progress with Google is optional. If you use it, Google Sign-In gives Firebase the email address of that Google account. The app shows the email on the Premium screen. You do not type a password into GubbiGo. If that Google account already has progress, the app asks before merging this device’s lessons into it.

Signing out clears lesson progress on this device and leaves the Google account’s saved progress in Firebase. Delete account on the Premium screen is available after you have saved progress with Google. It asks you to confirm with Google, then deletes the progress document, deletes the Firebase account, and clears lesson progress on this device. Purchases stay with the store. A new empty anonymous account then starts on the device.

6. Premium, seeds, and payments

Lessons spend seeds. Seeds refill over time, up to a small balance kept on the device. You can buy a seed pack for more seeds, watch an optional rewarded ad for one seed, or subscribe to Premium for unlimited seeds and no ads. Premium is optional. Lessons already on the device keep working without it, subject to the seed balance.

Google Play on Android, or Apple on an App Store build, charges, renews Premium, retries failed payments, and refunds. Seed packs are one-time purchases through the same store. We do not collect, store, or see your payment card number, UPI ID, or store-account password.

What leaves the device for a purchase

  • the store purchase or receipt, handled by Google or Apple;
  • that receipt, the Firebase account id, the product id, and whether Premium is active, so RevenueCat can tell the app the purchase is valid;
  • a local Premium status cached in GubbiGo after a successful check, used while you are offline;
  • for a seed pack, the Play order id stored only on this device. It is not uploaded with lesson progress.

We do not send your lesson scores, streak, or which lesson you finished to Google, Apple, or RevenueCat.

Renewal, cancel, restore

  • Premium renews until you cancel in Google Play (or the App Store, on an iOS build). Price, period, and renewal are shown before you pay.
  • If you cancel, Premium stays active until the end of the paid period. After that, seeds are limited again. Lessons already downloaded keep working.
  • Signing in with the same Google account on another device brings back the saved progress and checks Premium for that Firebase account. Restore purchases on the Premium screen checks the store account on the device. The purchase record stays with the Google or Apple account that paid.
  • A refund or chargeback turns Premium off after the store notifies RevenueCat and the app next checks (including on launch when online). A seed pack that was already added is not removed from the local balance by this app.

Manage or cancel in the store account, not inside a GubbiGo profile: Google Play subscriptions (Android) or Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions (iOS). Step-by-step cancel and restore is on Support. Payment details are also covered by the Google Payments Privacy Notice and, where applicable, Apple’s privacy policy.

7. Advertising

Learners who do not have Premium can choose to watch a short rewarded advertisement from Google AdMob. The reward is one seed. The offer is capped per day, and the count of ads watched stays on the device. There is no banner, and no advertisement is inserted between exercises. Skipping the ad leaves the lesson and the seed balance unchanged.

Premium includes no advertisements. While Premium is active, the app does not load an ad or use the advertising identifier for this purpose.

We do not sell your information. We do not give AdMob your name, email, lesson scores, or saved progress. You can limit ad personalization in your device settings (Google Settings → Ads, or the equivalent control on the device). Resetting or deleting the advertising ID is handled by the device, not inside GubbiGo.

8. Children

GubbiGo is designed so children can learn with a parent or guardian nearby. The app does not include chat, social features, or public profiles. It does not ask for a child’s name.

An install still creates a Firebase account id, and lesson progress can sync to it. That progress does not include a name or email unless someone chooses Save progress with Google. A parent or guardian should be the one to sign in with Google and to buy Premium or seed packs. Recurring Premium is billed to the store account until it is cancelled there.

RevenueCat receives the Firebase account id and purchase receipts when Premium or a seed pack is bought or restored. We do not send a child’s name, email, or lesson progress to AdMob. A parent should decide whether a child watches a rewarded ad. Premium turns those ads off.

If you believe we have received personal information from a child that we should not have, email developer@xlang.in and we will delete it if it is in our control (including asking RevenueCat to delete the corresponding customer record where we can).

9. App permissions

GubbiGo is built to use:

  • Internet — to download language packs, save and restore progress, sign in with Google, check for app updates, complete store purchases, check Premium with RevenueCat, and load an optional rewarded ad when Premium is not active;
  • Audio playback — to play lesson and word audio. The app does not record audio;
  • Advertising ID — only to request that optional rewarded ad. It is not used while Premium is active;
  • Local storage — to keep progress, downloaded lessons and audio, the cached Premium status, seed-pack order ids, and the day’s rewarded-ad count.

GubbiGo does not request microphone, camera, contacts, or precise location permission for these features.

10. Retention and deletion

  • On-device lessons: remain until you sign out, delete the account, clear app storage, or uninstall. Signing out or deleting a Google-linked account clears lesson progress on this device. The seed-pack order record stays, so those Play orders are not granted again. Uninstalling removes that record too. None of these steps cancel Premium.
  • Cloud progress: stays in Firebase until you delete the account in the app, or we delete it after a Delete my data request we can match. Signing out leaves the Google account’s saved progress in place.
  • Google email: stays on the Firebase account until that account is deleted. It is not stored in the progress document.
  • Store purchases: retained by Google or Apple under their policies. Cancel Premium in the store to stop renewal.
  • RevenueCat: keeps Premium status and purchase metadata for the Firebase account id. If you ask us to delete this, we will request deletion from RevenueCat where the product allows. The store will still have its own purchase record.
  • Rewarded-ad count: stays on the device and resets when lesson progress on the device is cleared. It is not saved in Firebase. Google may keep its own ad measurement records under its policy.
  • Download and update logs: Expo or Cloudflare may retain ordinary request logs under their policies.

11. Your choices and rights

You can:

  • learn without Premium, using the seeds on the device, and skip the optional ad;
  • skip Google sign-in and keep the anonymous account;
  • turn off network access; lessons already on the device still work offline;
  • clear app data or uninstall to remove local progress;
  • delete a Google-linked account from the Premium screen, which removes that account and its saved progress;
  • cancel Premium or request a refund through Google Play or the App Store;
  • use the Delete my data form (or email developer@xlang.in with the subject “Delete my data”) to ask what information we hold or to request deletion of an account we can match, including an anonymous one. Cancel and restore steps are on Support.

Depending on where you live (including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act and, where applicable, GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, or to withdraw consent. Purchase records held by Google, Apple, or RevenueCat are handled under those providers’ tools and policies; we will help where we can.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this page if we add banners or other ad placements, a payment method other than the official stores, analytics, or a new category of data. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Material changes will also be reflected in the store listing where required.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, parent requests, or to ask us to delete information we control, use the Delete my data form or email developer@xlang.in.

Please do not send payment card details or Google / Apple account passwords to this address. For billing disputes, use Google Play or App Store support as well.
