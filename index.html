How it works

You follow an adventure map of short lessons: listening, vocabulary, fill-in-the-blank, building real sentences in your language (the verb often comes last), and chats with the mascot Gubbi. Phonetic text sits next to the script so you can read along before you know the letters.

Early lessons are free. Later lessons unlock with optional Super, billed by Google Play or the App Store — not by a card form inside GubbiGo.

On-device progress

Scores, streaks, and downloaded packs live in a local database. Uninstalling the app deletes that data. It does not cancel Super.

Private by design

GubbiGo does not show third-party ads and does not ask for email sign-in. A parent or guardian can sit with a child without creating a GubbiGo profile.

Billed by the store

Super is sold and renewed by Google Play or the App Store. Payment details stay with the store, not with GubbiGo.
