How it works

You follow an adventure map of short lessons: listening, vocabulary, fill-in-the-blank, building real sentences in your language (the verb often comes last), and chats with the mascot Gubbi. Phonetic text sits next to the script so you can read along before you know the letters.

GubbiGo is free. Lessons use seeds that refill over time, and you can watch an optional ad for one more.

On-device progress

Scores, streaks, and downloaded packs live in a local database. Uninstalling the app deletes that data.

Private by design

GubbiGo shows only Google AdMob ads, requested as non-personalised and child-directed, and does not ask for email sign-in. A parent or guardian can sit with a child without creating a GubbiGo profile.

Nothing to buy

GubbiGo has no subscriptions or in-app purchases, and it never sees payment details.
