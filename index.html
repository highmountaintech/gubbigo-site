Learn Kannada on your device

GubbiGo is a language-learning app for structured Kannada. Lessons, audio, and progress stay on the phone. There is no account to create and no ads in the app.

About GubbiGo

You follow an adventure map of short lessons: listening, vocabulary, fill-in-the-blank, Subject–Object–Verb sentence building, and chats with the mascot Gubbi. Phonetic text sits next to Kannada script so you can read along before you know the letters.

Early lessons are free. Later lessons unlock with an optional Super subscription billed by Google Play or the App Store — not by a card form inside GubbiGo.

On-device progress

Scores, streaks, and downloaded packs live in a local database. Uninstalling the app deletes that data. It does not cancel Super.

No ads, no login

GubbiGo does not show third-party ads and does not ask for email sign-in. A parent or guardian can sit with a child without creating a GubbiGo profile.

Billed by the store

Super is sold and renewed by Google Play or the App Store. There is no card form inside GubbiGo.
