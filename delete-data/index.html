Privacy

Delete my data

Use this form to ask us to delete your GubbiGo account and the data we control. You do not need to be signed in to the app. We aim to complete a valid request within 30 days.

Uninstalling GubbiGo (or clearing app storage) deletes lessons, scores, and cached Super status on that phone. It does not cancel Super. Cancel Super in Google Play or Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

What we delete when you submit this form

If we can match your request to an account, we delete:

  • your GubbiGo sign-in (anonymous Firebase user, or Google-linked account);
  • cloud lesson progress stored for that user;
  • the Super / RevenueCat customer record we can control for that user (the store still keeps its own purchase history).

What we cannot delete from this form

  • On-device data — only you can remove it, by uninstalling or clearing app storage.
  • Super billing — Google or Apple keep the purchase record. Cancel auto-renewal in the store so Super does not renew.
  • Technical logs — Expo or Cloudflare may retain ordinary request logs under their own policies. See the Privacy Policy.

If we cannot find a matching account, we will still email you (at the address you give below) and tell you what we checked.

How to identify your account

If you tapped Save progress with Google, use that Google account email. If you never saved with Google, on-device data is already gone after uninstall; we may have little or nothing on our servers unless Super was bought on that install.

Do not send payment card details or Google / Apple passwords.

We use this to confirm the request and tell you when it is done.
