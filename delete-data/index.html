Privacy

Delete my data

Use this form to ask us to delete your GubbiGo account and the data we control. You do not need to be signed in to the app. We aim to complete a valid request within 30 days.

Uninstalling GubbiGo (or clearing app storage) deletes lessons, scores, and progress cached on that phone. On-device data is only removable by you, this way.

What we delete when you submit this form

We email a confirmation link to the address you give below. Click it, confirm on the page it opens, and if we can match the account we delete:

  • your GubbiGo sign-in (the Google-linked account tied to that email);
  • cloud lesson progress stored for that account.

What we cannot delete from this form

  • On-device data — only you can remove it, by uninstalling or clearing app storage.
  • Technical logs — Expo or Cloudflare may retain ordinary request logs under their own policies. See the Privacy Policy.

If we cannot find a matching account, the confirmation page will tell you so — there is likely nothing on our servers tied to that email.

How to identify your account

Use the Google account email you tapped Save progress with Google with. If you never saved with Google, your progress is stored under an anonymous account that has no email, so we cannot look it up here. It is deleted automatically after 90 days without opening the app, and on-device data is gone once you uninstall.

Do not send passwords.

The Google account you used with Save progress with Google, if any. We send the confirmation link here.

Email instead

Check your email. We sent a confirmation link to the address you entered. Click it within 24 hours to finish deleting your account. If you don't see it in a minute, check spam.
