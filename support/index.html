Help

Support

Email developer@xlang.in for app questions, parent requests, or deletion of data we control. Cancel Super in Google Play or the App Store — not in a GubbiGo profile.

Do not send payment card details or Google / Apple passwords. For a charge you do not recognize, use Google Play or App Store support as well.

How do I cancel Super?

Super is billed by Google Play or the App Store. Cancel auto-renewal here:

  • Android: Google Play subscriptions
  • iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions

After you cancel, Super stays on until the end of the period you already paid for. Free lessons keep working after that. See also the Terms of Use.

Super disappeared on a new phone

Sign in to the same Google or Apple account you used to subscribe, install GubbiGo, and use Restore purchases in the app while you are online. There is no GubbiGo login.

I lost my lessons

Progress is stored on that device. Clearing app storage, uninstalling, or switching phones without backing up the device can remove it. That does not cancel Super. Restore Super as above, then continue from free or unlocked lessons on the new install.

Audio will not play

Check that the phone is not on silent and that GubbiGo may play sound. If a lesson was just downloaded, wait until the pack finishes, then try again. Already-downloaded lessons still work with the internet off.

A language pack will not download

Packs need a network connection the first time. Stay on Wi‑Fi if you can, keep the app open until the download finishes, then try again. Lessons already on the phone work offline.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling deletes lessons on that phone. Super is cancelled only in Google Play or the App Store. To ask us to delete the anonymous Super record we can control, email developer@xlang.in with the subject “Delete GubbiGo data”. See the Privacy Policy for what stays with Google, Apple, Expo, or Cloudflare.
