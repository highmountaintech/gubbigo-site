Help

Support

Email developer@xlang.in for app questions or parent requests. To delete an account and data we control, use Delete my data.

Do not send passwords.

I ran out of seeds

Lessons use seeds. You hold up to five, starting a lesson uses one, and one comes back every 40 minutes. When you have none you can wait, or watch one optional ad for one seed (up to three a day). If no ad is offered, you are offline or the ad could not load; try again in a moment.

I lost my lessons

Progress is stored on the device. Clearing app storage, uninstalling, or switching phones can remove it. If you saved progress with Google (Home menu → Profile → Save progress with Google), install GubbiGo on the new phone and sign in with the same Google account to restore it. Progress in a guest (anonymous) profile cannot be restored after you uninstall.

Audio will not play

Check that the phone is not on silent and that GubbiGo may play sound. If a lesson was just downloaded, wait until the pack finishes, then try again. Already-downloaded lessons still work with the internet off.

A language pack will not download

Packs need a network connection the first time. Stay on Wi‑Fi if you can, keep the app open until the download finishes, then try again. Lessons already on the phone work offline.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling deletes lessons on that phone. If you saved progress with Google, use Home menu → Profile → Delete account in the app, or the Delete my data form. A guest (anonymous) profile has no email, so we cannot look it up; it is deleted automatically after 90 days without opening the app. See the Privacy Policy for what stays with Google, Expo, or Cloudflare.
